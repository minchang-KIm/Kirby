# Windsprig Credits

## Project

Windsprig: Echoes of the Gale was created as an original open-source game by Snowball_tree. Copyright (c) 2026 Snowball_tree. Source code and repository-authored game content are distributed under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

## Original generated art

All 52 shipped player, enemy, boss, world, and interface PNGs are original Windsprig project art generated from checked-in geometric recipes and deterministic seeds. No third-party character, logo, raster, or vector inputs are used. Exact recipes and decoded-pixel hashes are recorded in `assets/generated/art-provenance.json`.

## Original generated audio

All 28 music cues and 29 sound effects are original Windsprig project audio synthesized from checked-in composition, oscillator, envelope, and seeded-noise recipes. No third-party samples, recordings, or AI-generated audio are used. Exact parameters and PCM hashes are recorded in `assets/generated/audio-provenance.json`.

## Noto Sans KR

The Korean typeface is a deterministic static subset of Noto Sans KR from the pinned Google Fonts revision documented in `assets/LICENSES.md`. Noto Sans KR and the shipped subset are redistributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, retained at `assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansKR.txt`.

The complete file-by-file provenance ledger is available in `assets/LICENSES.md`.
