# Windsprig: Echoes of the Gale

Ride the living wind, harmonize enemy echoes, and restore six hand-crafted sky worlds in a storybook action-platform adventure for one to four local players.

Play in a desktop Chromium browser or download the Windows x64 release. Browser play requires a keyboard or compatible gamepad and a viewport of at least 1024×576. No account or telemetry is used; browser profiles stay in that browser, and Windows profiles stay under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Windsprig`.

## Store short description

Catch, launch, and harmonize living echoes across 30 hand-crafted stages in an original local action-platform adventure for one to four players.

## Feature proof

- Six storybook sky worlds, 30 stages, six multi-phase bosses, and 90 hidden Wind Motes.
- Six distinct echo abilities with deterministic combat, checkpoint, victory, defeat, and co-op rules.
- Keyboard and gamepad play, English and Korean, reduced motion, screen-shake control, hold/toggle actions, high-contrast redundant HUD cues, and independent volume controls.
- Local-only profiles with no account, telemetry, analytics, advertising, or server save.
