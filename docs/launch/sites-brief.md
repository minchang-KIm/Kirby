# Windsprig Sites Launch Brief

## Hero

- Eyebrow: **PLAY IN YOUR BROWSER · 1–4 LOCAL PLAYERS**
- Heading: **Catch the wind. Carry its echoes.**
- Body: Restore six storybook sky worlds in a kinetic action-platform adventure about movement, mastery, and the abilities you choose to carry.
- Primary CTA: **Play Windsprig**
- Secondary CTA: **Download for Windows**

Use the original Sprig key art and a world-one background crop as the focal image. The first viewport must show the product name, heading, body, both actions, input/platform note, and visible proof-strip entry without scrolling at 1440×900.

## Proof strip

30 hand-crafted stages · 6 multi-phase bosses · 90 hidden Wind Motes · Keyboard and gamepad · English and Korean

## Gameplay

Lead with the choice at the heart of Windsprig: draw a living echo into Sprig's vortex, launch it through enemies and hazards, or harmonize with it to carry a distinct ability. Show three concise cards—Draw, Launch, Harmonize—using real gameplay captures and no invented mechanics.

## Six worlds

Present one card for each canonical world in campaign order. Each card uses its approved world capture, localized name, one-sentence identity, signature mechanic, and boss name. Use stage three for the representative gameplay capture and the authored world palette for its accent.

## Local co-op

Explain one-to-four-player local play, explicit keyboard/gamepad joining, stable player identity, the shared weighted camera, distant-player guidance, checkpoint revival, and leader-confirmed goal gathering. Do not imply online multiplayer.

## Accessibility

List remappable native gamepad controls, active-device control reference, reduced motion, screen-shake control, draw/guard hold or toggle, master/music/SFX volume, mute, fullscreen/windowed play, integer scaling, high-contrast redundant HUD cues, and English/Korean support.

## Press kit

Provide the original logo treatment, social card, six world screenshots, one gameplay capture, fact sheet, credits, asset/font/audio provenance, support, privacy, source license, and version 1.0.0 release link. Every downloadable item must use its real checked-in or release-asset URL.

## Support and privacy

State that saves are local to the browser origin or `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Windsprig`, with no account, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or server save. Link directly to the canonical support, privacy, security, credits, and source pages.

## Visual system

- Ink: `#16202B`; paper: `#F5F7E2`; panel: `#0C1822`.
- Sprig mint: `#77DE99`; deep mint: `#36915B`; Sprig gold: `#F8C243`; sky cyan: `#70DEE8`.
- Typography: the checked-in Windsprig Noto Sans KR subset, with compact uppercase eyebrow text, bold rounded display headings, and highly readable body copy.
- Shape language: layered paper-cut sky contours, wind arcs, seed/leaf forms, rounded cards, crisp two-pixel-style outlines, and restrained gold focus rings.
- Motion: short wind-drift and reveal transitions that fully disable under reduced-motion preferences.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA text contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, useful alt text, 44 px minimum touch targets, and no color-only status meaning.

## Responsive acceptance

- 390×844: one-column content, sticky compact actions after hero, no clipped Korean copy, and press assets as a vertical list.
- 768×1024: two-column proof/gameplay/world cards where readable, with actions remaining above the fold.
- 1440×900: asymmetric hero, six-world grid, restrained maximum text measure, and no empty decorative bands.

The section order is fixed: Hero, proof strip, Gameplay, Six worlds, Local co-op, Accessibility, Press kit, Support and privacy, footer.
